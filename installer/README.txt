GETTING STARTED
  - Start EzDAWsIt from the Start menu. The first time, a short tour shows you round
    (take it again any time with Shift+F1).
  - Pick your audio interface in Setup > Audio device. ASIO drivers give the lowest delay.
  - Press F1 for every command and its keyboard shortcut.

WHERE YOUR WORK IS
  - Songs:  Documents\EzDAWsIt\Projects  (each song has its own folder)
  - Logs:   Documents\EzDAWsIt\Logs      (Help > Open the log folder)
  - EzDAWsIt saves as you work. If it ever closes unexpectedly, it offers to
    restore your unsaved changes the next time it starts.
  - Uninstalling EzDAWsIt never deletes your songs.

PLUGINS
  - EzDAWsIt hosts VST3 plugins. Scan them in Setup > Plugins.

REPORTING A PROBLEM
  - Help > Open the log folder, and send the newest log file with a description
    of what you were doing.
